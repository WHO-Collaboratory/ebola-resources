# Evidence Trackers

---

The evidence trackers below provide continuously updated surveillance of the scientific literature, evidence resources, and research activities related to the 2026 Bundibugyo virus disease outbreak.

| Resource | Description | Developed by | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bundibugyo Ebolavirus Evidence Surveillance** | A continuously updated evidence surveillance of Bundibugyo ebolavirus literature from **1 January 2026 onwards**, including peer-reviewed publications, preprints, and grey literature. Searches are run twice weekly across Embase, MEDLINE, Global Health, Scopus, and Europe PMC, with additional monitoring of publications from organizations such as WHO, CDC, ECDC, UKHSA, PHAC, and PAHO. | Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC) / Health Canada Library | [View evidence tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fm0TDAbSRjWQkL2X5OuSLz1kRr6a80nvpzQ8ubWMZZ4/edit?gid=0#gid=0) |
| **Bundibugyo Virus Research Hub** | A collaborative knowledge hub supporting locally led research, evidence generation, and capacity strengthening during the current outbreak. The hub provides research protocols, guidance, training materials, data collection tools, evidence resources, and opportunities for collaboration for researchers and outbreak response teams. | The Global Health Network (TGHN) | [View research hub](https://globalhealthoutbreaks.tghn.org/ebola/) |
| **Collaboratory Evidence Synthesis** | A curated collection of emerging evidence relevant to the 2026 Bundibugyo virus disease outbreak, including recent publications, preprints, evidence syntheses, guidelines, and other resources to support evidence-informed outbreak response. | WHO Collaboratory | [View evidence synthesis](https://who-collaboratory.github.io/ebola-resources/emerging-evidence/) |
