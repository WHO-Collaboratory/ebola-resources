Nearby countries, particularly Uganda, South Sudan, Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania, face the highest near-term risk of imported Bundibugyo virus cases from the DRC. Rankings for more distant countries vary across analyses, driven by differences in how each model treats air travel, land travel, and the assumed spatial distribution of source cases. Evidence suggests that surveillance is best concentrated on the highest-probability importation routes. Confidence is moderate, with relative destination rankings holding up better across analyses than the absolute probabilities attached to them.
