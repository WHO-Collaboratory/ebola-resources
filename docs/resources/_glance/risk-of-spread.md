Nearby countries, particularly Uganda, South Sudan, Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania, face the highest near-term risk of imported Bundibugyo virus cases from the DRC, and the importations into Kenya and Nigeria (via Uganda) as of 19 October fall within the higher-risk tiers, while rankings for more distant countries vary with how each model treats air travel, land travel, and the spatial distribution of source cases. Evidence suggests surveillance is best concentrated on the highest-probability importation routes. Confidence is moderate, with relative rankings holding up better across analyses than the absolute probabilities attached to them.
