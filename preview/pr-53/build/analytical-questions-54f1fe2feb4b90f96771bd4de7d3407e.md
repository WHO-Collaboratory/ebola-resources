# Analytical questions 

This section collects key analytical questions in this [shared google document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iSZ6-BsTrQnOHvX60Q7eFAjCrqRLp559dFdKzsGj2Rw/edit?gid=0#gid=0), open for additions, relevant to the 2026 DRC Ebola Bundibugyo outbreak response.

# Background 

The questions within [the document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iSZ6-BsTrQnOHvX60Q7eFAjCrqRLp559dFdKzsGj2Rw/edit?gid=0#gid=0) were compiled with input from individuals with operational and analytical experience in country-level response. No formal consensus or agreement process was conducted beyond these individual consultations. As such, the questions are intended to help guide and coordinate analytical efforts while reflecting suggestions from the wider community.

# Task

Please review the questions and indicate where you are currently working or planning to work by adding your information in [this link](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iSZ6-BsTrQnOHvX60Q7eFAjCrqRLp559dFdKzsGj2Rw/edit?gid=0#gid=0). This input will be compiled into an overview to help the community understand who is working on which topics and to identify potential gaps or bottlenecks. The objective is to support better coordination and enable more targeted collaboration where specific insights are needed for the response.

Please feel free to add lines in the [google sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iSZ6-BsTrQnOHvX60Q7eFAjCrqRLp559dFdKzsGj2Rw/edit?gid=0#gid=0) if the line corresponding to the question you want to answer is already used.

By contributing your information, you agree to it being shared on Collaboratory. If you have any questions, please contact [collaboratory@who.int](mailto:collaboratory@who.int).
